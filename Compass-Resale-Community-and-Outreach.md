# Compass: Resale Community and Outreach

Nov 5, 2026

## What Compass does

Compass puts a listed item in front of the people most likely to buy or share it: resellers, collectors and niche communities. It starts where Nextant stops. Nextant's Venue agent picks the marketplace; Compass drives extra demand to that listing from outside the marketplace.

Inputs from Nextant: the item record (identity, photos, copy), Ledger's price and floor, and Venue's chosen platforms.

Output: `channels {community_posts[], live_sale_slots[], lot_offers[]}`.

## Community and outreach playbook

- **Advertise where resellers are.** Bundles of low-value items sell best as a lot to *other resellers*: post lots in reseller Facebook groups, subreddit sell threads that allow it, and Discord flipping servers. Read each community's rules first; most ban unsolicited ads.
- **Collectors find collectors.** For items Nextant's Lens tags as collectible, post to the hobby's own forums and groups, where buyers pay for completeness and provenance.
- **Live selling for personality items.** Whatnot and TikTok Shop live streams move vintage and collectibles at prices static listings rarely reach. Compass batches suitable items into a show and schedules it.
- **Respect the delist lock.** Every outside post links to the live listing rather than taking a separate sale, so Nextant's inventory record stays the single source of truth.
- **Measure each community.** Track clicks, sales and the time each community costs, and drop the ones that do not pay back.
